Curiosity hooks for series
Use these recurring formats:

Do You Know Why? — Explain one everyday psychological pattern.

Have You Wondered? — Explore a common feeling people rarely discuss.

Are You Aware? — Reveal a subtle influence on behaviour.

The Psychology Behind… — Explain procrastination, ghosting, burnout, jealousy, or impulse buying.

Your Brain Is Not Broken. — Reframe a self-critical thought.

One Small Shift. — Give a realistic behaviour-change action.

Myth vs Psychology. — Correct a popular but inaccurate claim.

Turn a hook into a Reel
Hook: “Have you wondered why you replay awkward conversations at night?”
Explain: “Your brain may be trying to make sense of uncertainty and protect you from future embarrassment.”
Example: “You remember one awkward sentence, even though the conversation went fine overall.”
Action: “Try naming what you fear: ‘I am afraid they judged me.’ Then ask, ‘What is the evidence for and against that?’”
CTA: “Save this for the next time your mind will not let the moment go.”

Keep claims measured: say “one possible explanation,” “research suggests,” or “this may help,” rather than presenting a single study as a universal rule. APA recommends clear, accurate communication of psychological science, including appropriate context and limitations.
